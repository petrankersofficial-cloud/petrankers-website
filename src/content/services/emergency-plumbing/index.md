This is a sample service page. When something breaks, an emergency plumber stops the damage first and then fixes the cause. See our [service areas](/service-areas/) or read the [blog](/blog/) for tips on what to do while you wait.

## What counts as a plumbing emergency

Burst pipes, active leaks, no water, sewage backups, and a failed water heater in winter all need fast help.

## What to do before we arrive

Shut off the main water valve, turn off the water heater if it is leaking, and move valuables away from the water.
