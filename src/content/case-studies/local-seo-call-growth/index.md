## The challenge

The client ranked for almost nothing outside their own brand name. See our [blog](/blog/) for the playbook, or read the [sample post](/blog/how-this-blog-system-works/).

## What we did

- Built one service page per core service, using the City + ZIP formula
- Rebuilt the Google Business Profile categories and services
- Published two local guides per month

## The result

**Calls from Google went from 42 to 187 per month** within six months.
