FAQ

Questions we get all the time

If your question is not answered below, book a free strategy call. Most owners get their real answers in the first ten minutes of that call.

How long before my dog grooming business starts ranking?

Most clients see meaningful local pack movement inside 30 days, and consistent top-3 Google Maps rankings between 60 and 120 days. Speed depends on your starting point: an existing business with a claimed Google Business Profile and a working site moves faster than a brand-new business with no online footprint. Our average across 50+ pet businesses is 90 days to ranked, but we will give you a realistic timeline after the audit, not before.

How much does SEO for a dog grooming business cost?

Our packages start at [VERIFY: starting price] per month and scale based on the size of your service area and your competition level. A single-location salon in a smaller market costs less than a mobile grooming business covering an entire metro. Every quote is fixed and itemized. No hidden fees, no surprise upsells, no “performance bonuses” tacked onto your invoice.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Our standard agreement is month-to-month after an initial 90-day commitment. The 90 days exist because SEO needs that long to show meaningful results, and locking in lets us do the foundational work without a client pulling the plug at day 45 before any of the work has had time to show. After 90 days, you can leave with 30 days notice.

What is included in the monthly service?

Local SEO, Google Business Profile management, weekly GBP posts, monthly content , local citation building, review reply management, monthly rank tracking, and a written monthly report. The exact mix depends on your package and your goals. Everything is itemized in your contract.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No, and any agency that does is lying to you. Google does not allow ranking guarantees. What we will guarantee: full transparency on what we do, monthly reports you can audit, honest reasons when something is not working, and the ability to leave any time after the initial 90 days. Our 50+ pet business clients are the proof.

Why only pet businesses?

Because pet care has its own search behavior, its own seasonality, its own review dynamics, and its own conversion patterns. A generalist agency will use the same playbook on a roofer, a dentist, and a dog groomer. We will not. Our entire system, from keyword research to content calendars to GBP posting strategy, is built around how pet owners search and how pet businesses earn bookings.

Can you help if I don't have a website yet?

Yes. We build websites for pet businesses as part of our service stack. If you do not have a site, the first 30 days look different: we build the site, claim and optimize your Google Business Profile, and lay the SEO foundation in parallel. Most new businesses are live and ranking inside 60 to 90 days from kickoff.

How do you report progress?

A written monthly report, sent on the first business day of every month. The report includes: what we did the previous month, ranking changes for your target keywords, your Google Maps position trend, calls and bookings driven by SEO, and what is queued for next month. The report is yours to keep. No login required.
